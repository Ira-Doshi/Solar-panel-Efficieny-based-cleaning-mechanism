# Solar-panel-Efficieny-based-cleaning-mechanism

Accumulated dust on solar panels severely impacts their efficiency and results in considerable energy loss, particularly in outdoor locations that are extremely dusty. Conventional cleaning techniques are generally done manually, are inefficient, and are not designed for use in ongoing maintenance. This paper discusses and presents a fully autonomous solar panel cleaning system that includes real-time performance monitoring through utilization of an ESP32 microcontroller and INA219 sensor. This system continuously measures panel output and environmental factors and only activates cleaning operations when it has been determined that the efficiency of the solar panels is significantly below optimum levels for the ambient level of sunlight that is received. In order to deliver the required cleaning, a motor-operated mechanism is integrated with a pump to provide water flow across the solar panel, therefore effectively removing surface contaminants. Laboratory and field test results confirm that dust accumulation can devalue the output of a solar panel by as much as 40%. Using this system, the output of the solar panels would be restored by up to 90-92% of the original performance, thereby resulting in an overall net positive energy balance because the total energy consumption of the entire system is less than the amount of energy that would have been lost through the accumulation of dust. This system provides an efficient, scalable, and cost-effective solution for maintaining photovoltaic generation efficiency.
